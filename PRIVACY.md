# Infinite IPTV privacy policy

_Last updated: 6 October 2026_

Infinite IPTV is a video player. It does not provide any channels, movies, series or other content. You
sign in with the details of an IPTV subscription you already have, and the app plays what that provider
offers you.

## What the app stores

- **Your provider details.** The server address and username you enter are saved on your computer when you
  choose "Keep me signed in". The password is saved in Windows Credential Manager. You can delete a saved
  account from the sign-in screen at any time.
- **Logs.** The app writes a local log file for troubleshooting. It stays on your computer.

## What the app sends, and to whom

- The app connects only to the provider server you enter, to sign in, load channel lists, programme guides,
  artwork and the streams you choose to watch.
- The app has no analytics, telemetry, advertising or crash reporting, and the developer has no server that
  receives your data. Nothing you enter is sent to the developer or to any third party.

Your provider's own privacy policy applies to the data it receives when you use its service.

## Children

The app is not directed at children. What is shown depends entirely on the provider you sign in to.
